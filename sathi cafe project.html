Welcome to Sathi Cafe

A new chapter in Janakpur's culinary scene

Janakpur's New Premium Destination

Located in the heart of Bajrang Chowk, Janakpur, Sathi Cafe brings the premium cafe culture of Kathmandu right to your neighborhood. We are not just a cafe — we are a gathering place for friends, a workspace for creatives, and a sanctuary for tea lovers.

Our mission is simple: serve the best tea in Janakpur alongside mouthwatering momos, flavorful chowmein, and our signature Mango Hookah experience. Every dish is crafted with passion, every cup brewed to perfection.
